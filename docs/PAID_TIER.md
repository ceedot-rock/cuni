# Free vs paid (first offer)

**Tagline:** Critical rules that must mean the same in every runtime — or they don’t ship.

## Three bullets

1. **Free — public Studio**  
   Try exactness in the browser, run the spend demo, publish into the shared stub. No account required. Good for proof and demos.

2. **Paid — private Studio workspace**  
   Your policies stay yours: save rules, exactness history, publish/register audit trail. Built for teams that need a record of what passed and when.

3. **Later — API**  
   `check` + `register` for CI and agents. Same gate, embeddable. Not the first charge.

## What you are not selling first
Compiler licenses, a standalone Rider cloud, or a generic “security suite.”
